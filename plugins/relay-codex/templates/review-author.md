## Exact response units

One reply per top-level inline root linking each relevant original request; one general PR comment for general comments and review bodies with original URLs. Show each unit's full text, item IDs and root. No responses means no completion comment. Result placeholders are `{{commit}}` and `{{verification}}`.

## Actual outcome
